CAPSTONE DESIGN · 2026

Site-Specific Wireless Channel Modeling & Probabilistic Shaping

A simulation-based study combining site-specific 3D ray tracing with probabilistic constellation shaping to investigate communication performance in multi-user interference channels.

AREA

Wireless Communications

TOOLS

Python · Sionna RT · TensorFlow · Blender

FREQUENCY

28 GHz

01 · OVERVIEW

Project Overview

The goal of this project was to construct a site-specific wireless channel model and use the resulting channels to study communication performance under interference.

A 3D model of an outdoor environment around the engineering buildings at Yonsei University was created and used in Sionna RT to simulate wireless propagation.

Multiple transmitter and receiver configurations were evaluated to construct 2×2 interference-channel matrices.

02 · CHANNEL MODELING

Site-Specific Ray Tracing

The environment geometry was reconstructed using photographs and 3D modeling tools, then simplified in Blender for use in electromagnetic ray-tracing simulations.

The resulting scene was imported into Sionna RT and evaluated at 28 GHz. Line-of-sight and reflected propagation paths were used to estimate complex channel coefficients.

Example ray-tracing scene with transmitter and receiver positions.

03 · INTERFERENCE CHANNEL

2×2 Channel Modeling

Channel coefficients extracted from the ray-tracing simulation were organized into a 2×2 complex channel matrix.

The received signals were modeled as

Receiver 1

Y₁ = h₁₁X₁ + h₂₁X₂ + N₁


Receiver 2

Y₂ = h₂₂X₂ + h₁₂X₁ + N₂

The diagonal terms represent the desired links, while the off-diagonal terms represent cross-user interference.

04 · PROBABILISTIC SHAPING

Optimizing 16-QAM

Instead of assigning equal probability to all 16-QAM symbols, the probability distribution of each user's constellation was optimized.

Gradient-based optimization with Adam was used to adjust symbol probabilities according to an objective that considered mutual information and transmission power.

Example optimized probability distribution over a 16-QAM constellation.

05 · RESULTS

MI–Power Trade-off

Different optimization weights produced different symbol probability distributions.

When mutual information was emphasized, the shaping distribution favored communication performance. When the power penalty was increased, probability mass shifted toward lower-energy constellation points.

Key Observation

Probabilistic shaping can control the trade-off between mutual information and transmission power in a multi-user interference channel.
